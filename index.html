03 / HOW IT WORKS

Understand before you provide.

The qualification timer starts only after admin approval, not when a user submits a form.

01

Create and verify your account

Register with a strong password and verify your email address.

02

Submit LP evidence

Provide the DEX position URL, transaction hash, LP wallet and eligible USD tier.

03

Wait for manual review

An authorized program admin checks the position and records approval or rejection.

04

Maintain your LP for six months

After the qualifying period and a further position check, you may request reward withdrawal. Early removal may forfeit the reward under final terms.

05 / IMPORTANT RISKS

Know the risks. Stay in control.

01

Impermanent loss

Price changes between paired assets can reduce position value compared with simply holding.

02

Smart-contract risk

DEX pools, tokens and wallets may contain vulnerabilities or be affected by exploits.

03

LP position risk

Concentrated liquidity may go out of range and stop earning fees.

04

No guaranteed return

LP rewards and trading fees are not guaranteed. Review the final program terms.
