HELP CENTER

Frequently asked questions

Can I withdraw my LP before 180 days?

No. The deployed locker contract requires the position to reach its unlock timestamp before LP withdrawal.

Are LP trading fees paid as a separate claim?

The locker does not expose a separate LP-fee harvesting function. Trading fees accrue in the PancakeSwap V2 pair reserves and can affect the value of the underlying LP position. The actual withdrawal amount depends on pool conditions.

Can I lock less than $50?

Yes. Any positive USDT and RND amounts accepted by PancakeSwap can create a 180-day locked LP position. The $50 threshold applies only to reward calculation; below one full matched $50 step, the user reward is zero.

How are RND rewards calculated?

The contract reads the current PancakeSwap V2 reserve-ratio spot price, compares the actual paired USDT and RND value, and awards 20 RND for each full $50 matched step.

How does the referral work?

Open the website through a friend's referral link (it looks like …/?ref=0x…) and add liquidity. The referrer is recorded on your first registration and is permanent: a wallet first registered without a referrer cannot attach one later. To receive referral rewards yourself, connect your wallet, press "Activate referral link" if shown, and share your link.

When can rewards be claimed?

Rewards mature at the same unlock timestamp as the associated LP position. Claims also require enough RND in the locker reserve for that position's payout.

I opened the link from WhatsApp but the wallet does not connect

Tap Connect Wallet and choose your wallet app (MetaMask, Trust Wallet, TokenPocket or SafePal). The site reopens inside that wallet and connects automatically. If it does not open, choose "Copy website link" and paste the link into your wallet's DApp / Browser tab.

Does the website hold my funds?

No. Transactions are signed by your wallet and executed by the smart contract. Never share your seed phrase or private key with anyone.
