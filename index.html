03 / HOW IT WORKS

Understand before
you provide.

Use only official DEX interfaces and verify the token contract, network and exact pool before signing any transaction.

01

Prepare your wallet

Use a self-custody wallet, select BNB Smart Chain where applicable, and keep enough native BNB for network gas.

02

Verify the RND pool

Confirm the official RND contract address, paired asset, pool fee, pool version and any applicable hooks. Similar-looking pools may be different pools.

03

Choose your position carefully

Concentrated-liquidity pools require a price range. If the market price moves outside your range, your position may stop earning swap fees.

04

Review every transaction

Check token amounts, approvals, network fees and transaction details in your wallet. Never share your seed phrase or private key.

05 / IMPORTANT RISKS

Know the risks.
Stay in control.

Providing liquidity involves market and smart-contract risks. Only participate after you understand how the pool works and can afford potential losses.

01

Impermanent loss

Changes in the relative prices of the paired tokens can leave you with less value than simply holding them.

02

Out-of-range positions

Concentrated liquidity may stop earning fees when the market price moves outside your selected range.

03

Smart-contract risk

DEX pools, tokens, wallets and any future lock contract may contain vulnerabilities or be affected by exploits.

04

No guaranteed returns

Trading fees vary and token prices can fall. Proposed community rewards are subject to final terms and availability.

06 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is a liquidity provider (LP)? +

A liquidity provider supplies token assets to a decentralized exchange pool so traders can swap between them. LPs may receive a share of eligible swap fees, depending on pool rules and active liquidity.

Is $100 the minimum? +

The proposed community program minimum is $100 in eligible LP value. This is a proposed program condition and must be confirmed in the final published terms before enrollment opens.

Are the six-month RND rewards guaranteed? +

No. The reward tiers are currently a proposal. They become an enforceable program only after the final rules, reward reserve, lock mechanism and claim process are deployed and verified.

Will I earn DEX trading fees? +

You may earn eligible swap fees while your liquidity is active and in range. Fees vary with trading volume, pool fee settings and your share of active liquidity. They are not guaranteed.

Can I add liquidity on both DEXs? +

Potentially, if both pools are verified and supported by the final program rules. Each DEX position is separate and has its own risks, fees and management requirements.
